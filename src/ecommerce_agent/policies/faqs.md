# Store FAQs

### How long do I have to return an item?
You can request a refund within 30 days. The refund is sent to the payment provider that took the original payment.

### Can I get a partial refund?
Yes. A partial refund is allowed up to the amount still refundable on the order.

### Which items cannot be refunded?
Gift cards, digital downloads, and items marked final sale are non-refundable.

### What happens if my refund is above 50?
A refund above 50.00 in the order currency alerts support. No refund is sent until support approves it.

### Where does the refund go?
The refund goes back through the original payment provider. The store does not send a refund to a different card or bank account.

### Do you store my card number?
No. Card numbers stay at your payment provider's checkout. Do not type a card number into this chat.

### How does currency conversion work?
A conversion quote comes from the payment provider connected to this store. The quote is not a completed payment. Creating the converted payment is done by the store backend.

### How does settlement work?
Settlement reads what the payment provider has captured or paid out. Capturing an authorized payment requires the store backend.
